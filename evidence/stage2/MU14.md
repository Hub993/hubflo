# MU14 — Conversation-State Lifecycle Policy

Source dependency: `29fcfd9` (accepted MU1–MU13)

Authority: Package 1.2, Annex A 1.0, and `HUBFLO_MU14_AUTHORITY_VALUES.md`.

## Implemented capability

- 24-hour UTC expiry at the exact `>= last_activity + 24 hours` boundary.
- Persisted `last_activity_at`, retirement timestamp, and retirement reason.
- State-relevant continuation attempts update activity; reads and deterministic
  unrelated-command bypass do not.
- Atomic lifecycle retirement for `cancel`, `start over`, and `never mind`.
- Exact abandonment normalization: surrounding whitespace and letter case only.
- Consistent lifecycle storage for legacy await and persistent clarification.
- Conversation retirement does not execute a business-object lifecycle handler.
- Existing atomic continuation claim and legacy await compatibility remain in
  place.

## Schema migration

Existing `conversation_states` tables receive nullable, backfilled lifecycle
columns through the existing startup repair mechanism:

- `last_activity_at TIMESTAMP`
- `retired_at TIMESTAMP`
- `retirement_reason VARCHAR(32)`

Fresh databases receive the same columns through SQLAlchemy metadata.
